Presentation

Yozaru (from ヨザル meaning Night monkey) is a masculine Utau voicebank created by Yoazur avaliable in multiple languages.
It is highly recommended to use these voicebanks using OpenUtau for ease of use. Sounds best between C3-A4, but is versatile with high pitched songs.

Character description

Yozaru is a teenager who loves music and singing. He can be shy but he becomes more confident when he sings. He is however quite lazy and is often sleepy, almost nocturnal (explaining the meaning of his name). That said, he is smart and he has experience in computer science. Fun fact, he uses a walkman to listen to music, but he doesn't really want people to know that (it's his character item).

Latest Voicebank: Yozaru Fullmoon

Fullmoon is a trilingual OpenUTAU voicebank recorded in E3 using the m2RUg reclist by Utau French Ressources. It has 2 voice colors: Natural and Soft.

Though it is mainly for French use, it does have Japanese Hiragana and English compatibily through the use of a custom dictionnary. Use French through the FR VCCV phonemizer and Japanese / English through the EN X-SAMPA phonemizer. Japanese mostly works well by itself but English might need to be a little creative with phoneme/alias replacement (sorry about that lol).
